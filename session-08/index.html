Paper layer · revised in Version 2

A region can participate in more than one organized whole

Question

When a painted droplet participates in a plant, a reflection, a water cycle, and a composition, what makes any one boundary more than arbitrary?

Observation

The page directly shows that one authored SVG region can receive several simultaneous memberships without changing its pixels.

Interpretation

Plural belonging need not mean unconstrained belonging. A material process, a visual relation, and a compositional role supply different reasons for drawing a boundary.

Limitation

The interaction demonstrates possible assignments inside one authored image. It does not establish which categories every viewer should accept, or settle a general theory of objects.

Current claim · Version 2

A part can belong to several wholes when each membership names a defensible relation, not merely a resemblance.
