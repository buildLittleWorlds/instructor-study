Today’s course practice

Can a first-time reader use the page without its maker standing beside it?

Students will conduct a sound-off encounter, observe the reader’s path, and revise labels, ordering, or instructions. One reader supplies evidence about one encounter—not a verdict on the app.

Planned instructor delta

An original microfiction with relational characters

A 120–180-word passage will be divided into selectable fragments. Readers can assign each fragment to Character A, Character B, their relationship, the setting, or several categories. The interaction will ask how a character can be distributed across words that also construct someone or something else.

Activation requires factual evidence:
  • a completed Version 2 visual interaction;
  • a sound-off reader encounter with observable actions;
  • one reader-path revision tied to that observation;
  • clear separation between the reader’s behavior and Daniel’s judgment.
