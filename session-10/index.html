Today’s course practice

Document responsibility through the actual build record

Students will trace a consequential choice through chats, commits, screenshots, tests, rejected changes, and their own judgment. The instructor example must use its real Session 7–9 record rather than a polished fictional provenance story.

Planned AI contribution

A second observer, not an answer key

Readers will annotate passage fragments first. Only then may they load a small browser-local embedding model and compare fragments with predeclared character, relationship, and context anchors.

Cosine values will be labeled model similarity, never confidence, truth, attention, or proof that a fragment belongs to a character. Flat or strange results will remain evidence rather than being tuned away.

Activation requires factual evidence:
  • a completed Version 3 literary interaction and real sound-off revision;
  • an actual project provenance trail through Versions 1–3;
  • verified live and baked embedding measurements from one pinned model revision;
  • a meaningful non-model experience when loading fails.
