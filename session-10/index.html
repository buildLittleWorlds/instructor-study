Epistemic boundary · what the current project can claim

The glider still does not compose alone.

Computed and recordedB3/S23 generates each displayed Life state. The browser derives sound and exports from the chosen settings. Git records the named repository states and timestamps.
Designed and judgedThe seeds, board bounds, pitch maps, scales, timbres, chance ranges, arrangement, stopping points, prose, sources, and project lock are human-directed choices implemented with AI assistance.
Still openWhether listeners hear one musical identity, how much agency belongs to a procedure or performer, and whether the final work is compelling beyond its usefulness as an authorship experiment.

Chance boundary. Prepared chance answers inside a field somebody designed. It does not make Conway’s rule random, erase the mapper, imitate Cage, or turn a browser into an autonomous composer.
